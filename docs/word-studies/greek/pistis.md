# πίστις (Pistis)

## Greek Term
**πίστις** — Transliterated as *pistis*. Commonly translated as "faith," "trust," or "reliance."

## Lexical Meaning
- Classical Greek: trust, confidence, reliability
- Hellenistic Jewish usage: fidelity to God, covenant faithfulness
- New Testament usage: often translated as "faith," describing belief, trust, and loyalty

## Contextual Notes
- Appears frequently in Paul’s letters (e.g., Romans, Galatians)
- Key for understanding justification and salvation debates
- Differs subtly from modern English understanding of “faith” as mere belief

## Theological Implications
- Connects trust in God with covenant obedience
- Central to Pauline theology
- Explains distinctions between "faith" and "works" in the New Testament
