# λόγος (Logos)

## Greek Term
**λόγος** — Transliterated as *logos*. Commonly translated as "word," "reason," or "principle."

## Lexical Meaning
- Classical Greek usage: reason, principle, speech
- Hellenistic Jewish usage: divine wisdom or agent of creation
- New Testament usage: particularly in John 1:1 and related passages

## Contextual Notes
- Appears in John 1:1: "In the beginning was the Word (λόγος)..."
- Philosophical background in Stoicism and Hellenistic thought
- Frequently discussed in early Christian theology (e.g., Church Fathers)

## Theological Implications
- Connects God’s creative power and rationality
- Bridges Jewish and Hellenistic concepts
- Central to understanding Christology in the Gospel of John
