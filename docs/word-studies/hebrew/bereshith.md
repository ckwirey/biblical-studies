# בְּרֵאשִׁית (Bereshith)

## Hebrew Term
**בְּרֵאשִׁית** — Transliterated as *bereshith*. Commonly translated as "in the beginning."

## Lexical Meaning
- Root: רֵאשִׁית (re’shith) meaning "beginning" or "first"
- Refers to temporal or priority beginning
- Used in Genesis 1:1 to describe the start of creation

## Contextual Notes
- Opening word of the Hebrew Bible
- Sets the tone for cosmology and divine action
- Hebrew grammar: preposition “בְּ” + “ראשית” = “in [the] beginning”

## Theological Implications
- Establishes God as the origin of creation
- Frames the narrative of divine order and sovereignty
- Connects to later theological discussions about creation ex nihilo
