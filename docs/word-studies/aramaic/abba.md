# אבא (Abba)

## Aramaic Term
**אבא** — Transliterated as *abba*. Commonly translated as "father."

## Lexical Meaning
- Intimate, familial term: “father,” “dad,” or “papa”
- Used in Jewish Aramaic speech as a term of personal address

## Contextual Notes
- Appears in Mark 14:36 and Romans 8:15
- Conveys deep intimacy and trust in God
- Differs from the formal Hebrew “אָב” (av) in tone

## Theological Implications
- Highlights personal relationship with God
- Important in Christology and prayer studies
- Shows continuity between Jewish familial language and Christian usage
