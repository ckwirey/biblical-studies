# Biblical Studies

This site is a public collection of notes and essays focused on the academic
study of the Bible.

Topics include:
- Biblical canon and textual history
- Word studies in Hebrew and Greek
- Theological interpretation
- Historical and cultural context
- Engagement with modern questions where Scripture is relevant

The goal is clarity, accuracy, and careful reasoning grounded in primary sources.
